Privacy Policy

Game Dropaboom (Android app th.dropaboom.game and web version) · Developer: Samart Nueangnil · Last updated 7 Oct 2026

Summary

  • The game itself has no accounts or sign-in and does not ask for or collect your name, email, contacts, location or other personal data.
  • The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device data to show and measure ads (details below).
  • The web version in a browser has no ads.

Game data stored on your device

  • Progress (scores, coins, skins, items, quests, stats, player level, play streak, badges, daily challenge and event results) and settings are stored only on your device (app/browser local storage). We do not receive it. You can delete it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.
  • We do not use analytics and do not track you across apps or websites.

Advertising (Android app) — Google AdMob

  • The Android app uses Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.) to show a banner at the bottom of the screen, an occasional full-screen ad between games, and optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch (for example to double the coins from a finished game).
  • To show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud and abuse, Google may collect and process data such as device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID and app set ID, IP address (which can indicate an approximate location), device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version), diagnostic information (for example app and SDK performance) and ad interaction data (ads shown, taps, views).
  • This data is collected directly by Google through the Google Mobile Ads SDK and is handled under Google's own policies. We (the developer) do not receive it in a form that identifies you. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are used, and you can choose. Where required, you can change your choice later from the app. Elsewhere you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Security & privacy → Privacy → Ads).
  • Rewarded ads are always optional. The game never gives rewards for sharing or inviting friends.

Notifications (Android app)

  • The app can show a local reminder notification (for example after about 2 days without playing) that a welcome-back gift is waiting. It is scheduled on your device only; no data is sent to us or to a server to do this.
  • Android asks for notification permission only after you have finished a game. You can turn reminders off in the game's Settings (Reminders: Off) or in Android settings at any time.

Other device features

  • Vibration is used only for in-game haptics. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Sharing happens only when you tap Share / Challenge / Invite. Your device's share sheet opens and you choose the app and the person; a score image is drawn on your device and only leaves it if you share it. Sharing never gives in-game rewards.
  • Backup save (Settings) creates a text code on your device that you can copy and keep yourself. Nothing is uploaded.
  • There are no online leaderboards, chats or cloud saves.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If we change how the game handles data, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
